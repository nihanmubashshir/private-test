A minimal, dark, mobile-first system. Tokens are delivered as Tailwind v4 @theme CSS variables on a numeric ramp, with every component below specified in all of its states. Auth screens for US-001 are applied at the end.

07 — Rules for the implementer

Non-negotiables

320px floor
No horizontal scroll at 320px. Auth card is w-full max-w-md inside px-4.
44px targets
Every button, toggle and link-as-button is at least 44px tall; form buttons are full width on mobile.
16px inputs
Never below text-base on any input, or iOS zooms the page on focus.
Safe areas
Top bar pads with env(safe-area-inset-top), sheets and toasts with the bottom inset.
One accent
Gold is reserved for the primary action, focus rings and the QR/authenticator link. Never decorative.
Mono for machines
Secrets, codes, ids and CLI commands in DM Mono. Never in Manrope.
QR quiet zone
The QR image sits on white with 16px padding, at least 200×200, inside a <details> on mobile.
Errors are generic
Never say which field was wrong. One role="alert" above the submit button.
